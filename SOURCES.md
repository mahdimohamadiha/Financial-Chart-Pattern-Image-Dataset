## Relationship Between Source Data and This Dataset

The original CSV financial time-series data are third-party source material and are not claimed as original work by Mahdi Mohammadiha.

This dataset consists of a separately created and curated collection of derived chart images produced through:

- Selection of source Forex time-series data.
- Rule-based financial pattern detection.
- Chart rendering.
- Manual inspection and verification.
- Selection and curation of final examples.
- Dataset organization and labeling.

The ownership statement for this dataset therefore applies to the original dataset-specific processing, selection, curation, labeling, and organization performed by Mahdi Mohammadiha, and does not transfer or claim ownership of the underlying third-party raw data.
